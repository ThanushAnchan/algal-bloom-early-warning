================================================================================
END-TO-END DATAFLOW LIFECYCLE (7 TO 14 DAY DETECTION TIMELINE)
================================================================================

DAY 0 - BASELINE (Clear Water):
  Satellite NDCI < 0.15 | Dissolved Oxygen: 7.5 - 8.5 mg/L
  Status: NORMAL (Green)
  Action: Routine automated 5-day satellite revisit scanning.

DAY 1 TO 3 - NUTRIENT INFLUX & GERMINATION:
  Sewage / agricultural fertilizer runoff enters village tank.
  Micro-algae begins division. Surface reflectance changes slightly in Red Edge band.
  Satellite NDCI: 0.18 - 0.25 | DO: 7.5 mg/L
  Status: NORMAL -> WATCH (Yellow)
  Action: System flags tank in Panchayat Watch List.

DAY 4 TO 6 - RAPID BLOOM ACCELERATION (CRUCIAL INTERVENTION WINDOW):
  Algae reaches exponential growth. Water turns green/scum begins forming.
  Satellite NDCI: 0.26 - 0.38 | Daytime DO: High (photosynthesis), Night DO: Declining
  Status: HIGH ALERT (Orange)
  Action: AUTOMATED ALERT TRIGGERED to Cooperative Head & Panchayat Development Officer.
          Recommended SOP: Deploy emergency pump aerators; stop upstream runoff.

DAY 7 TO 10 - BLOOM PEAK & INITIAL DIE-OFF:
  Nutrients become exhausted; algae dies and sinks to sediment.
  Aerobic bacteria multiply exponentially to decompose dead matter.
  Satellite NDCI: 0.40 - 0.55 (Peak) | DO begins rapid drop: 5.5 -> 4.2 mg/L
  Status: CRITICAL HYPOXIA RISK (Red)
  Action: Emergency night aeration (02:00 AM - 07:00 AM) and quicklime dosing.

DAY 11 TO 14 - OXYGEN COLLAPSE (IF NO INTERVENTION):
  Dissolved oxygen breaches lethal limit (< 4.0 mg/L), dropping to 1.5 - 2.5 mg/L.
  Result: 100% fish mortality and total economic collapse (Rs. 1.5 - 3 Lakh loss/tank).
  With Early Warning System: Aeration deployed on Day 5 prevents oxygen drop.
