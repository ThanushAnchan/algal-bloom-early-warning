================================================================================
SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE: VILLAGE FISH TANK ALGAL BLOOM EARLY WARNING SYSTEM
State Scope: Karnataka (3,407 Gram Panchayat Tanks)
================================================================================

1. PROBLEM SUMMARY
------------------
- Fish tanks face recurring mass fish mortalities triggered by toxic algal blooms.
- Bloom formation requires 7 to 14 days before crashing dissolved oxygen (< 4.0 mg/L).
- No village-level monitoring currently exists; loss is irreversible upon detection.
- Target: Detect bloom in Days 1-4 via freely available satellite imagery and alert farmers.

2. HIGH-LEVEL THREE-TIER STRUCTURAL BLUEPRINT
----------------------------------------------
[TIER 1: SATELLITE & SENSING LAYER]
  ├── Sentinel-2 MSI Multi-Spectral Imagery (Bands: B4-Red, B5-Red Edge, B8-NIR)
  ├── Karnataka State Remote Sensing Applications Centre (KSRSAC) Water Bodies
  └── IMD Weather / Heatwave / Agricultural Runoff Telemetry

[TIER 2: ANALYTICAL & RISK SCORING LAYER]
  ├── Cloud & Shadow Masking (SCL Band Filtering)
  ├── Normalized Difference Chlorophyll Index (NDCI) Calculator
  ├── 14-Day Bloom Growth Velocity & Trajectory Analyzer
  └── Dissolved Oxygen Crash Predictor (Hypoxia Early Warning Engine)

[TIER 3: DISSEMINATION & ACTION LAYER]
  ├── Multi-Channel Alert Gateway (Kannada SMS, WhatsApp, Automated Voice Call)
  ├── Gram Panchayat & Fisheries Department GIS Web Dashboard
  └── Field Advisory SOP Engine (Mechanical Aeration, Water Flushing, Liming)
