================================================================================
PIPELINE SPECIFICATION: DISSOLVED OXYGEN DELETION & HYPOXIA MODEL
================================================================================

1. PHYSIOLOGICAL BASIS
----------------------
- Safe DO Level for Indian Major Carps (Catla, Rohu, Mrigal): > 6.0 mg/L
- Stress / Cessation of Feeding Threshold: 4.0 - 5.0 mg/L
- Lethal Asphyxiation / Mass Fish Mortality Threshold: < 4.0 mg/L (Critical < 2.0 mg/L)

2. MULTI-FACTOR PREDICTOR INPUTS
--------------------------------
- Primary Factor: 7-day rate of change in NDCI (dNDCI / dt)
- Secondary Factor: Water surface temperature (higher temperature lowers oxygen saturation)
- Tertiary Factor: Consecutive overcast / cloudy days (halts daytime photosynthetic DO replenishment)
- Quaternary Factor: Tank average depth & surface area to volume ratio

3. PREDICTIVE WARNING LEAD TIME
--------------------------------
- Trigger Condition: When NDCI > 0.25 and daily increase rate > 0.03/day
- Output: 48-Hour and 72-Hour projected Minimum Dissolved Oxygen (mg/L)
- Intervention Benefit: Delivers 4 to 6 days of actionable lead time before lethal 4.0 mg/L crash.
