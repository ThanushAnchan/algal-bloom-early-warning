================================================================================
PIPELINE SPECIFICATION: SATELLITE DATA INGESTION
================================================================================

1. DATA SOURCE SPECIFICATION
-----------------------------
- Satellite Platform: Copernicus Sentinel-2A / Sentinel-2B MSI (Multi-Spectral Instrument)
- Product Level: Level-2A (Bottom-Of-Atmosphere surface reflectance)
- Spatial Resolution: 10m to 20m per pixel (suitable for village tanks > 1 hectare)
- Temporal Resolution: 5-day revisit cycle over Karnataka coordinates
- Data Cost: Free & Open Access (Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem / Google Earth Engine)

2. SPECTRAL BAND ALLOCATION
----------------------------
- Band 4 (Red, ~665 nm): Chlorophyll absorption baseline
- Band 5 (Vegetation Red Edge, ~705 nm): Primary reflectance peak of algae blooms
- Band 8 (Near-Infrared, ~842 nm): Land-water boundary separation and floating scum detection
- Band SCL (Scene Classification Layer): Identifies cloud, cirrus, cloud shadow, and water

3. PRE-PROCESSING FILTERING STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------
Step 1: Ingest village tank boundary polygon from Karnataka GeoRegistry.
Step 2: Buffer polygon inward by 10 meters to eliminate shoreline soil contamination.
Step 3: Query imagery acquired within the last 5 days.
Step 4: Discard scenes with tank cloud coverage > 25%.
Step 5: Extract mean pixel reflectance across water mask.
