================================================================================
PIPELINE SPECIFICATION: ALGAL BLOOM DETECTION & SPECTRAL INDICES
================================================================================

1. SPECTRAL INDICES FORMULAS
----------------------------

A. Normalized Difference Chlorophyll Index (NDCI):
   Formula: NDCI = (Band_5_RedEdge - Band_4_Red) / (Band_5_RedEdge + Band_4_Red)
   Purpose: Directly isolates Chlorophyll-a concentration in turbid shallow water.

B. Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI):
   Formula: NDVI = (Band_8_NIR - Band_4_Red) / (Band_8_NIR + Band_4_Red)
   Purpose: Differentiates between submerged algae vs dense surface floating scum/weeds.

C. Normalized Difference Turbidity Index (NDTI):
   Formula: NDTI = (Band_4_Red - Band_3_Green) / (Band_4_Red + Band_3_Green)
   Purpose: Distinguishes inorganic suspended sediment from organic algal growth.

2. THRESHOLD CLASSIFICATION MATRIX
-----------------------------------
Index Value Range   | Chlorophyll-a Est. | Water Condition | Alert Status
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
NDCI < 0.15         | < 15 ug/L          | Clear / Normal  | NORMAL (Green)
0.15 <= NDCI < 0.25 | 15 - 35 ug/L       | Elevated Nutrients| WATCH (Yellow)
0.25 <= NDCI < 0.40 | 35 - 75 ug/L       | Moderate Bloom  | HIGH ALERT (Orange)
NDCI >= 0.40        | > 75 ug/L          | Hyper-Eutrophic | CRITICAL (Red)
